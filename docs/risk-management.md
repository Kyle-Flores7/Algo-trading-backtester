# Risk Management

**Status: not yet implemented.** No position sizing, stop-loss, or risk rules exist in this codebase today — strategies only decide direction (`Signal`), not size or risk.

## Possible future areas (undecided)

- Position sizing
- Per-trade risk limits
- Max drawdown limits / circuit breakers
- Stop-loss / take-profit rules
- Portfolio-level exposure limits

No specific percentages, thresholds, or rules have been chosen — decide and document these deliberately when risk management is actually designed, not by default.
